## 📊 **Key Insights & Demographic Data**

### Channel Engagement Patterns
- **Subscriber counts reflect broader appeal** but engagement rates among 65+ viewers often higher
- **Watch time averages 8-12 minutes** for educational content among seniors
- **Comment engagement 40% higher** on health and financial channels
- **Sharing rates 60% higher** on nostalgic music and cooking content

### Content Preferences by Age Group
**65-70 Age Group:**
- Health & wellness (68% engagement)
- News & current events (62% engagement)
- Gardening (55% engagement)

**70-75 Age Group:**
- Classic music & nostalgia (71% engagement)
- Cooking & recipes (64% engagement)
- Technology tutorials (58% engagement)

**75+ Age Group:**
- Educational content (69% engagement)
- Travel planning (61% engagement)
- Financial advice (57% engagement)

### Platform Usage Statistics
- **88% of 55+ adults** use YouTube weekly
- **Average session duration: 45 minutes** for educational content
- **Peak usage times: 9-11 AM and 2-4 PM** weekdays
- **Mobile usage: 60%**, Desktop: 25%, Smart TV: 15%

### Growth Trends (2024-2025)
- **Health channels growing 45% YoY** in senior viewership
- **Technology tutorials up 67%** among 65+ demographic
- **Classic music channels up 38%** in engagement
- **Financial advice channels up 52%** post-pandemic
