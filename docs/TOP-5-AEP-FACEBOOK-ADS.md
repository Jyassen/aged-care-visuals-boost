#### **PRODUCTION NOTES**

**B-Roll Requirements:**

- Senior actors (65-75 age range) with authentic expressions
- Medicare letters/documents (generic, CMS-compliant)
- Kitchen table/home office setting (warm, relatable)
- Advisor/representative in professional but approachable setting
- Phone call interaction (staged but natural)

**Infographic Elements:**

- Calendar transition animation (2025 → 2026)
- Statistics display (15+ years, 1000+ families)
- Map highlighting Nassau County
- Checkmark icons for benefits
- Simple, clean design (blues/greens, professional palette)

**Text Overlay Specifications:**

- Font: Clean, readable sans-serif (minimum 24pt for mobile)
- Color: White text with subtle dark outline OR dark text on light background
- Placement: Bottom third of screen (safe zone for mobile)
- Animation: Fade in/out, slide up, or simple appear
- Duration: Each overlay visible 2-4 seconds

**Voiceover Direction:**

- Tone: Empathetic, understanding, not condescending
- Pace: Conversational, not rushed (allows for pauses)
- Emphasis: "EVERY year", "don't have to", "no-cost", "clear answers"
- Avoid: Salesy tone, pressure, jargon

**CMS Compliance Checklist:**

- ✅ Uses "no-cost" not "free"
- ✅ No fear-based urgency tactics
- ✅ Factual claims only (15+ years, 1000+ families)
- ✅ Educational approach prioritized
- ✅ No outcome promises
- ✅ Deadline mentioned as information only
