## 📊 FULL ANALYSIS SUMMARY

### **Customer Insights:**

**Key Takeaways from Stage 1:**
1. **Trust is the #1 barrier** - All personas want to see the actual broker (name/photo)
2. **"No-cost" needs explanation** - Creates suspicion without compensation model clarity
3. **Mobile optimization critical** - Shorter paragraphs, 30-second video cuts needed
4. **Fear-based messaging backfires** - AD #3 original scored lowest (7/10)
5. **Empathy wins** - AD #1 and #4 scored highest on emotional resonance

**Persona-Specific Insights:**
- **Margaret (overwhelmed):** Responds to validation, needs reassurance

- **Linda (cost-conscious):** Motivated by savings, needs proof
- **Frank (frustrated):** Motivated by AGAINST something, values authenticity
- **Patricia (caregiver):** Shares content for parents, wants simple explanations
