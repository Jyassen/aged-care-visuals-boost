## 🎯 SCORING METHODOLOGY

### Success Probability Score (0-100)

**Formula Components:**
1. **Emotional Resonance** (25 points) - Does it connect emotionally?
2. **Clarity** (20 points) - Is the message immediately clear?
3. **Trust Signals** (20 points) - Does it build credibility?
4. **Action Motivation** (15 points) - Strong CTA with clear value?
5. **Target Fit** (10 points) - Matches persona needs?
6. **CMS Compliance** (5 points) - Risk-free messaging?
7. **Mobile Optimization** (5 points) - Works on small screens?

**Prediction Tiers:**
- **90-100:** WINNER - Launch immediately with major budget
- **80-89:** STRONG PERFORMER - Launch with moderate budget
- **70-79:** SOLID - Test with smaller budget, optimize
- **60-69:** NEEDS WORK - Refine before launch
- **Below 60:** DON'T RUN - Major issues
