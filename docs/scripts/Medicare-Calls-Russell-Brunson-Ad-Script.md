# Russell Brunson Style Ad Script: "The Call"
## Raw, Authentic, Direct Response

---

## **VIDEO SPECS:**
- **Style:** "Raw" / User Generated Content (UGC) feel.
- **Camera:** Handheld or stable selfie-style.
- **Tone:** Urgent, Helpful, Authentic, Authoritative but personal.
- **Target:** Seniors confused by the noise of Medicare season.
- **Format:** Vertical (Reels/TikTok/Shorts) or Square (FB/IG Feed).

---

## **SCRIPT:**

### **[0-5 seconds] - THE PATTERN INTERRUPT (HOOK)**
**VISUAL:** 
- Camera pans across a desk or workspace.
- Shows you (Jamal) actively on the phone, maybe pointing at a screen or looking at paperwork. 
- It looks busy. Real work is happening.
- **Audio:** Ambient sound of a busy office or you finishing a sentence on the phone ("Okay, we'll get that taken care of, Mrs. Johnson. Hold on just one second.").

**ACTION:**
- You put the phone on hold or down on the desk.
- You turn and look **directly** into the camera lens.

---

### **[5-15 seconds] - THE PROBLEM (AGITATE)**
**VISUAL:** 
- Close up, direct eye contact. 
- Handheld camera movement is okay (adds to the "raw" feel).
- **Text Overlay:** "Confused by Medicare?" (Bottom center)

**VOICEOVER (Direct Address):**
*"This time of year we help hundreds of seniors find a plan that suits their specific needs..."*

*(Slight pause, lean in)*

*"But you need to make sure you've considered the **entire** situation before you choose a plan."*

---

### **[15-25 seconds] - THE CRITICAL DETAILS**
**VISUAL:** 
- You tick these off on your fingers or use emphatic hand gestures.
- **Text Overlay (Pop up on screen):** 
  - 🩺 Doctors
  - 💊 Medications
  - 💰 Copays

**VOICEOVER:**
*"Doctors. Medications. And important copays."*

*"This year there are so many companies out here, it’s difficult to know which one to choose."*

---

### **[25-35 seconds] - THE SOLUTION & CTA**
**VISUAL:** 
- Warm smile. Reassuring demeanor.
- Gesture to yourself/the logo on your shirt if visible.
- **Text Overlay:** "YourMedGuy.com" or "Link in Bio"

**VOICEOVER:**
*"Let us at YourMedGuy help!"*

*"Fill out the form. Ask for Jamal!"*

**ACTION:**
- You pick the phone back up to get back to work.
- **Fade to Black / Logo.**

---

## **PRODUCTION NOTES:**

### **Filming Tips:**
- **Lighting:** Natural light is best. Don't make it look like a studio. It should look like you just took a break from saving people money to record this.
- **Audio:** Use a lapel mic if possible for the direct address, but the "ambient" start can be camera audio. The voice needs to be crisp and authoritative.
- **Wardrobe:** Casual professional or branded "YourMedGuy" gear.

### **Russell Brunson "Hook-Story-Offer" Breakdown:**
- **Hook:** Seeing the "behind the scenes" of the work (Making calls).
- **Story:** The struggle of the season (Hundreds of seniors, too many choices).
- **Offer:** The help (YourMedGuy) + The Call to Action (Fill form, Ask for Jamal).
