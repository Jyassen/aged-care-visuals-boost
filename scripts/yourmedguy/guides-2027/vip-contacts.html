02Who to call

Medicare VIP Contacts

2027 Edition

The numbers I give people across New York City and Long Island every week. Save them in your phone. Every one of these is free, and none of them will sell you anything.

Federal Medicare
Medicare Helpline1-800-MEDICARE(1-800-633-4227)

Open 24/7 for enrollment help, coverage questions and plan information. Ask for a representative, and have your Medicare number ready.

Medicare TTY1-877-486-2048

Same help for people who are deaf or hard of hearing.

Medicare.govmedicare.govmedicare.gov/plan-compare

The official site. The Plan Finder compares every Medicare Advantage and drug plan in your ZIP code, with estimated yearly costs based on your medications.

Social Security1-800-772-1213ssa.gov/medicare

Sign up for Parts A and B, replace a Medicare card, question a Part B premium or income-related charge (ask about Form SSA-44), and apply for Extra Help with drug costs.

Free counseling, no sales
HIICAP (New York's SHIP)1-800-701-0501

Free, unbiased Medicare counseling from trained counselors across New York State. Good for a second opinion on any plan decision.

Senior Medicare Patrol (NY)1-800-333-4374

Report suspected Medicare fraud, billing errors or scams. If a caller asks for your Medicare number, hang up and call this line.

NYC Aging Connect212-AGING-NYC(212-244-6469)

New York City's one-stop line for older adults: benefits screening, home care, meals, caregiver support and local senior centers.

03Money help

Programs that pay part of the bill

I meet people every week who qualify for help and never knew it. If you're close to a limit, apply anyway.

New York money help
EPIC1-800-332-3742health.ny.gov/health_care/epic

New York's prescription program for people 65 and older. Works with your Part D plan to lower what you pay at the pharmacy. Income up to $75,000 single or $100,000 married.

NY Medicaid Helpline1-800-541-2831health.ny.gov/health_care/medicaid

Medicaid and the Medicare Savings Programs, which can pay your Part B premium and more. New York has no asset test for the Medicare Savings Programs.

NY State of Health1-855-355-5777nystateofhealth.ny.gov

New York's health insurance marketplace. Helps with Medicaid applications and coverage before you turn 65.

Extra Help (Part D)1-800-772-1213ssa.gov/medicare/part-d-extra-help

Lowers your drug plan premium, deductible and copays. In 2027 you pay no more than $5.80 for a covered generic and $14.40 for a covered brand. Resource limit for 2027: $18,710 single, $37,340 married.

New Jersey neighbors
NJ SHIP1-800-792-8820

New Jersey's free Medicare counseling program.

PAAD and Senior Gold1-800-792-9745

New Jersey's prescription help programs. PAAD drugs cost $5 for generics and $7 for brand names.
