01Before you sign anything

10 questions to ask before you
choose a Medicare plan

2027 Edition

Every plan looks good in the brochure. These are the questions I ask on every call, so you find out what a plan really costs before you're enrolled. Bring this sheet to any plan conversation, including one with me.

1

What are my copays for a primary care visit and a specialist visit?

Most 2027 New York plans charge $0 for primary care, but specialist copays run about $35 to $55 a visit. If you see specialists often, this number matters more than the premium.

2

What will each of my medications cost on this plan?

Same drug, different plan, very different price. Ask for the tier and copay of every drug you take. In 2027 the most you'll pay for covered drugs is $2,400 for the year, and many plans charge a drug deductible of up to $700 on brand-name tiers.

3

Are my doctors in the network at the office I actually go to?

Network status can differ by location. Call the doctor's office and ask which plans they take for 2027, then confirm it with the plan.

4

Is there a medical deductible or a drug deductible?

Some plans have a $0 medical deductible and a separate drug deductible. Know what you pay before the plan starts paying.

5

What is the yearly out-of-pocket maximum?

This is your safety net in a bad year. Many 2027 New York plans set it between about $7,000 and $9,850 for in-network care. Lower is better, if the rest of the plan fits.

6

Do I need a referral to see a specialist?

HMOs usually need one, PPOs usually don't. Decide how much freedom you want, and what you're willing to pay for it.

7

What needs prior authorization?

Scans, procedures, some drugs and some therapy often need approval first. Ask for the list, so you're not surprised when you need care.

8

What do the extra benefits really cover, and what are the limits?

"Dental" can mean cleanings only, or real work like crowns and dentures. Ask for the yearly dollar maximum on dental, vision, hearing and any spending card.

9

What happens if I'm admitted to the hospital?

Many plans charge a daily copay for the first several days of a stay, often $300 to $500 a day. Multiply it out so you know the real number.

10

What is the plan's Star Rating, and did anything change for 2027?

Medicare rates plans from 1 to 5 stars for quality and service. Ask for the 2027 rating, and ask what changed from last year's plan: premium, copays, drug list, network.

One more: "Can you show me that in the plan's Summary of Benefits?" If the answer is only in a sales pitch, it's not an answer.
